Who We Are

Experience-led international sourcing.

Based in Busan, Republic of Korea, Asian Resources Co. is an independent sourcing and trading business connecting overseas suppliers with opportunities in the Korean market.

Led by John Jeon, ARC draws on more than 30 years of international business experience in manufacturing, materials, and cross-border commercial coordination.

Practical. Responsive. Relationship-driven.

We focus on understanding buyer requirements, identifying suitable supply partners, and coordinating clear commercial communication from initial inquiry through transaction discussions.
